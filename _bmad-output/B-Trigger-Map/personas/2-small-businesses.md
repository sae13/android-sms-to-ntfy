# Persona: Small Businesses

## Summary
Small business owners seeking low-cost communication forwarding to internal ticketing or notification systems.

## Priority Reasoning
Need to forward business communications to internal systems

## Positive Drivers
- Cost-effectiveness
- Integration with internal systems
- Scalability

## Negative Drivers
- Security concerns
- Lack of support
- Downtime risk

## Goals and Tasks
- Successfully forward SMS/calls
- Receive replies via SSE
- Configure via dashboard
